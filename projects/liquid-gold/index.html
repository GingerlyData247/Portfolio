PROJECT / FALL 2024

Liquid Gold

Project Snapshot

Role: Unity Expert, Developer

Tools: Unity, Slack, GitHub

Team: 7 people total, 2 developers

Overview

This was a professional project done for a client that included a written contract. the idea of the project was to develop a demo for a farming disillation simulation game that we would develop for a studio, to potentially continue development for the project if they got funding.

This was a 3D isometric game where you, similar to stardew valley, had a farm, grow crops, use those crops to distill alcohol, and sell to customers. there where multiple buildings that had to be built and linked together to distill farmed crops. each crop had a randomness of quality and how much could drop from farming a crop. using higher quality materials would make higher quality alcohol, which sells for more money. there was a day and night cycle for crops to grow and mature, and each day you had to water the crops or they would die. There was a general market for buying and selling, and an inventory for the player where they would store their crops, alcohol, and seeds.

My Role

  • Created a 3D Isometric farming game in Unity where you harvest crops, distill alcohol, and compete in a market against rival companies to make money from alcohol.
  • Was a Professional group project work for a client over 3 months where I was the programmer and Unity Expert.
  • Put peoples assets into the game, organized, and converted models and sounds.
  • Worked on Crafting, Farming system, Player money, sound effects, Inventory functionality, Market functionality, and scriptable objects for the crops and crafting recipes.
